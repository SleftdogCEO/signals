# Sleft Signals — Concierge Launch Playbook

The goal of this phase is NOT to sign up users or seed a database. It is to
**hand-broker the first 5-10 real referral relationships yourself** and learn
exactly which referral lane is painful enough that physicians engage. Build
real edges between practices that genuinely want each other, then let those
relationships tell you what to build and scale.

You are the unfair advantage: a Tampa EM resident with physician relationships.
Start there.

---

## Step 1 — Build your first-contact list (target: 10-15)

People who will actually take your call or text:
- Residency colleagues and attendings you're friendly with
- Med-school classmates now in practice
- Physicians in your family / personal network
- Anyone who already owes you a favor or likes you

Drop each into `outreach-tracker.csv` with status `To contact`.

---

## Step 2 — The opener (low commitment, learning-first)

Do NOT pitch a platform. Ask to learn.

> "Hey Dr. ___, I'm building something to help practices get more patients
> through doctor-to-doctor referrals instead of burning money on ads. You'd
> actually be perfect to learn from — can I steal 10 minutes to ask how
> referrals work in your practice? Not selling you anything."

Text or call beats email for warm contacts.

---

## Step 3 — The discovery call (the real work)

Ask these and write the answers into the tracker. The answers ARE the product.

1. Roughly what share of your new patients come from other physicians vs ads,
   walk-ins, or insurance lists?
2. Which specialties send you the most patients today? (their current sources)
3. Which specialties do you *wish* sent you more? (their referral gaps)
4. When you refer a patient out, where do they go? Do you have someone you
   trust for that, or do you just hand them a list and hope?
5. Have you ever lost a patient or felt stuck because you had nowhere good to
   send them?
6. Telehealth check: are there services you'd refer to even if they aren't
   local — weight loss / GLP-1, psychiatry, hormone therapy?
7. If I introduced you to a complementary practice that specifically wants
   your referrals, would that be useful?

Question 7 is the close. A "yes" is your first edge.

---

## Step 4 — Capture and multiply outward

Every practice they *name* in answers 2, 3, and 4 is a warm recruit. Log them
in the tracker under "Next Practices To Recruit." Your pitch to each:

> "Dr. ___ mentioned they'd love to connect with a practice like yours for
> referrals. Mind if I make the intro?"

That is how the network grows along real referral lines instead of scraped
strangers. One conversation should produce 2-4 named next targets.

---

## Step 5 — Broker the intro (do it by hand)

When two practices both want the connection, send one warm three-way email:

> Subject: Intro — Dr. A (Cardiology) <> Dr. B (Primary Care)
>
> Dr. A and Dr. B — connecting you two directly. You both see [shared patient
> type] and each mentioned wanting a trusted referral partner on the other
> side. Dr. A handles [X]; Dr. B sends a steady stream of [Y]. I'll get out of
> the way — grab 15 minutes and see if it's a fit. Happy to help however I can.
>
> Grant

Log it as `Intro made`. When it produces an actual patient, that's
`Active referral` — your first proof point.

---

## What success looks like for this phase

- 10+ discovery conversations logged
- A clear answer to "which referral lane is most painful + cash-rich"
- 3-5 brokered intros
- At least 1 referral relationship that produces a real patient

Hit that, and you'll know the exact niche to build SEO around, what data the
matching actually needs (including telehealth/state-licensure lanes), and
whether practices will eventually pay. THEN we seed and scale.

---

## Notes that will shape the product

- **Geography is not universal.** In-person specialties (ortho, derm, imaging,
  surgery) match by local radius. Telehealth / cash-pay lanes (GLP-1, telepsych,
  hormones) match by state licensure, not distance. Track `Telehealth` and
  `States Served` per practice now so the matching model can split later.
- **Niche down for SEO.** Pick the one lane the conversations surface as most
  painful. Generic "physician referral network" ranks for nothing; a specific
  lane ("where Florida PCPs send patients for medically supervised weight loss")
  ranks and converts.
